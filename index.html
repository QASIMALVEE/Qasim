AlgoLab, a DSA Visualizer ↗

Team project

A website that animates more than 15 data structures and algorithms one step at a time. It covers 5 sorting algorithms, 7 graph algorithms including BFS, Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford and Kruskal, and trees like BST, AVL and B-Tree.

You can play, pause, step backwards and change the speed. Every comparison and swap is narrated as it happens, and each algorithm shows its time and space complexity. Each structure is its own module, so the team could build new ones in parallel. It uses Konva.js on a Ruby on Rails backend and ships in Docker.

AI for a Reactor-Separator Process

Course project

A full study of a plant with two stirred-tank reactors and a flash separator, done for Application of AI in Chemical Engineering. I modelled it from first principles, trained AI models on it, then used them to optimise and control the plant.

It also showed where the AI falls short. The PI loop still handled a sudden drop in feed temperature better, and the learned dynamic model drifts when it predicts far ahead.

  • Python
  • SciPy
  • scikit-learn
  • Fuzzy models
  • Genetic algorithm
  • MPC

  1. Catalytic Synthesis of 4-Methoxypropiophenone

    Prof. Sumit Kamal

    Helped make 4-methoxypropiophenone by Friedel-Crafts acylation of anisole with propionic anhydride, using Amberlyst-15 as a solid acid catalyst.

    • Ran the reaction in a temperature-controlled four-neck glass reactor with steady stirring.
    • Took samples over time to track how much product formed at different catalyst loadings.
    • Heterogeneous catalysis
    • Batch reactor
    • Amberlyst-15

    Prof. Sumit Kamal
